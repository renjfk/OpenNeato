> [!CAUTION]
> **This release migrates the filesystem from LittleFS to SPIFFS.** On first boot after OTA update, the data partition is automatically reformatted. All logs and cleaning history sessions are erased. WiFi credentials and settings (NVS) are not affected. Use the session import/export feature added in v0.5 to back up cleaning history before updating.

### Breaking Changes
- Switch filesystem from LittleFS to SPIFFS - all logs and cleaning history are wiped on OTA update (341a1f3)

### Bug Fixes
- Fix serial communication progressively degrading after days of continuous uptime due to LittleFS metadata fragmentation from repeated directory traversals (341a1f3)
